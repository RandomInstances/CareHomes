Choosing a care home shouldn't start with a search.

It should start with an assessment. A clinician sees your relative in person — then we shortlist the homes that can meet the need we found, arrange the visits, and stay with you to the move. One fee of LKR 50,000 covers all of it.

Step 1

Medical evaluation

A registered clinician visits and assesses your relative in person — physical and mental. Nothing is decided from a phone call or a web form.

Step 2

Shortlisting properties

We match that assessment against homes our own team has visited, and shortlist only the ones that can genuinely provide the care required.

Step 3

Placement

We arrange the visits, coordinate with the home, and stay with you through the move.

LKR 25,000 to begin · 25,000 on placement · We reply on WhatsApp

What our team checks on every visit

We visit each home ourselves before it can receive a placement. These are observations, recorded and dated — not an endorsement, and never a score.

Staffing

Night-shift nursing

We ask for a number, not "enough", and we see the roster. Nights are when falls, breathing trouble and confusion happen.

Medical cover

Doctor arrangement

Who the doctor is, how quickly they can attend, which hospital the home transfers to, and who travels with the resident.

Money

What the fee excludes

We collect the fee schedule in writing. Medicines, diapers, physiotherapy, private rooms and hospital escorts are the usual extras.

Registration

Registration sighted

We ask to see the National Secretariat for Elders certificate and the date of the last inspection, and record exactly what we saw.

Evidence

Photographed by us

Rooms, kitchen and bathrooms, photographed by our team on the day — not supplied by the home.

Everyday life

Daily programme

What residents actually did the previous afternoon. A specific answer means there is a programme. A vague one usually means television.
